# Sprint 201: compare connected product outcomes by period

The portfolio period-comparison response now includes source-product outcomes for each currency. For every connected source it reports paid and refunded amounts and their shares of the corresponding same-currency portfolio totals in each period, plus net paid amounts and share changes in percentage points when both period denominators exist.

The results use campaign-linked commerce events from the selected authorized organization units. Source shares are descriptive composition measures, not causal attribution. A share is unavailable when the corresponding portfolio total is zero or missing; currencies are never combined.
