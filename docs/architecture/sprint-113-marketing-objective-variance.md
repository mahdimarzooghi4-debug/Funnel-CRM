# Sprint 113: Marketing objective variance foundation

Campaign target portfolio reports now include additive variance summaries for impressions, clicks, leads, and conversions, both by group and across the selected portfolio. For campaigns with observed actuals, the report compares actual and target on the same campaigns. Targets belonging to campaigns with no observed actual remain in a separate unobserved-target total.

These figures are arithmetic differences and coverage summaries, not explanations of cause. Currency metrics and ratios are excluded from additive aggregation. Existing period, tenant, organization-scope, and campaign limits remain in place.
