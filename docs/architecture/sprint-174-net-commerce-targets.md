# Sprint 174: net commerce targets

Campaign target plans can define a minimum full-period net-paid-to-spend ratio in one currency. Evaluations use explicitly campaign-linked paid events minus refunded events, divided by recorded spend in that same currency.

The goal appears in individual target evaluation, the campaign decision workspace, and portfolio outcomes. Access to its calculated value requires commerce-event viewing permission. It remains a descriptive operating measure, not accounting profit or causal attribution.
