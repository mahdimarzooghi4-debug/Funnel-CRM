# Sprint 176: net commerce outcomes in campaign portfolio

The campaign target portfolio now includes paid, refunded, net paid, net amount per paid order, and pooled paid-to-spend multiples by currency. Campaign totals remain visible in each item, with grouped and full-portfolio values calculated from summed amounts and spend rather than averages of campaign ratios.

Commerce outcomes are returned only for organization scopes where the viewer has commerce-event permission. The ratios are descriptive and use explicitly campaign-linked events.
