# Sprint 173: net commerce in the campaign decision workspace

The campaign decision workspace now shows net paid amount, net amount per unique paid order, and period net-paid-to-spend for each currency alongside existing targets and gross paid-to-spend.

The period ratio uses full-period totals and is distinct from the experiment metric that pairs days with both commerce events and spend. These are recorded, descriptive amounts; no accounting profit, currency conversion, or causal attribution is inferred.
