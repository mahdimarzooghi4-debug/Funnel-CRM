# Sprint 84: explicit sales stage outcomes

Opportunity pipeline stages may be configured as open, won, or lost. Existing stages without an outcome remain open, preserving prior behavior. The sales forecast applies manager-configured probabilities only to open-stage opportunities and reports won/lost counts separately; terminal opportunities do not inflate the active gross or weighted pipeline amounts.

Counts are scoped to the requested expected-close-date period. This sprint does not add an actual close date, loss-reason taxonomy, or infer outcomes from stage names; those remain future work.
